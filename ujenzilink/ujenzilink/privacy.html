Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025

UjenziLink is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and email address provided in intake or contact forms.
  • Project details including location, type, stage, and budget range.
  • Site visit documentation including photographs taken at your site.
  • Communications between you and UjenziLink (WhatsApp, email, phone notes).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To schedule and conduct your project review.
  • To prepare and deliver your review report.
  • To communicate with you about your engagement.
  • To improve our services and internal processes.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal data to third parties — including contractors, construction professionals, or any other parties — without your explicit consent, except where required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain project records for a period of 3 years after the conclusion of an engagement, after which they are securely deleted. You may request earlier deletion of your data.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@ujenzilink.co.ke.

6. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Site visit photos are stored securely and linked only to your project record.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related enquiries, contact: privacy@ujenzilink.co.ke
