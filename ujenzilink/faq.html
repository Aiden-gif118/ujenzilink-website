About the Service

An UjenziLink reviewer visits your site in person. During the visit, they photograph and document what they find, measure progress against your agreed plans or bill of quantities, assess material quality where visible, and note any risks or discrepancies. A full written report is delivered to you within 48 hours of the visit.
UjenziLink is available to any owner with a construction project in Kenya. This includes homeowners building for personal use, diaspora Kenyans building from abroad, families managing multi-generational builds, and small developers managing rental or commercial projects. If you need independent, verified oversight of a build in Kenya, we can help.
Yes. You can engage UjenziLink at any stage — before the first sod is turned, halfway through walling, or even after you've noticed something concerning. A Project Review will give you an honest assessment of where things stand right now, regardless of how far along the project is.
Our Project Review plan is designed exactly for this. It's a single, standalone review with a full written report. There's no subscription or ongoing commitment required. Many clients start with a one-time review and then choose to continue with stage-based or monthly oversight once they see the value.
We contact you within 24 hours of receiving your intake form to confirm details and schedule the site visit. After the visit, your report is delivered within 48 hours. From submission to report, most clients receive their findings within 4–7 days depending on scheduling.
