How It Works

From site visit to confident decision — in 5 clear steps.

UjenziLink turns site complexity into a clear, actionable report. Here is exactly what happens when you engage us.

01

You share your project details

Complete our intake form with your project location, stage, and main concerns. The more context you give us, the more targeted your review will be. Takes about 5 minutes.

02

We assess the risks and confirm scope

Our team reviews your submission, identifies the key risk areas for your project type and stage, and contacts you within 24 hours to confirm the review scope and schedule.

03

We verify the site in person

An UjenziLink reviewer visits your site. They document what they find with photos and measurements, assess progress against agreed plans or BOQ, check material quality, and note any risks or discrepancies.

04

We create your report

Within 48 hours of the site visit, you receive a clear, written report. It covers what was found, what is outstanding, what risks exist, and exactly what we recommend regarding any pending payments.

05

You make your decision

The decision is always yours. You now have independent, verified information to support it. Pay, hold, or request more work — with confidence backed by evidence.

What You Receive

Every report includes:

Site photo documentation

Timestamped photos from the actual site visit, showing progress, materials, and issues found.

Stage progress assessment

A clear rating of how complete each stage is, relative to what was agreed or expected.

Risk flags

Specific issues identified — material quality, workmanship gaps, discrepancies with plans — with severity ratings.

Payment recommendation

A clear, plain-English recommendation on whether to release, hold, or partially release pending funds.

Next steps

What work should be completed before the next payment or next review is appropriate.

Debrief call (optional)

Available for clients who want to walk through the report findings verbally before acting.
