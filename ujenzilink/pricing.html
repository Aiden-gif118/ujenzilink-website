What UjenziLink does not include

We are an oversight and decision-support service. We do not replace your contractor, engineer, lawyer, or any construction professional.

Fund custody or holding

We never receive, hold, or release client funds. Your money stays entirely in your control.

Construction contracting

We do not hire, manage, or instruct contractors or laborers on your behalf.

Physical construction or labor

We do not carry out any construction work, supply materials, or manage a site.

Legal or engineering replacement

Our reports are advisory. We are not a substitute for a licensed structural engineer or legal counsel.
