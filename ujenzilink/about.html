About UjenziLink

Built because trust alone is not enough.

We started UjenziLink because we watched too many families lose money, time, and hope on construction projects that should have worked.

Why We Exist

The trust problem that nobody was solving.

In Kenya, most construction happens through relationships built on trust. A homeowner trusts a contractor. A contractor trusts a foreman. A foreman trusts suppliers. When that chain works, it works well. When it breaks — and it often does — the owner is the one who pays.

The homeowner who saved for 12 years, the Kenyan in Manchester sending money home every month, the family building their late mother's plot in the village — these are real people who deserve more than hope and a prayer that the contractor they've hired is honest.

We built UjenziLink to be the independent layer between good intentions and hard evidence. Not to replace trust — but to verify it.

Our Mission

To give every homeowner, diaspora builder, and small developer in Africa access to independent, trustworthy construction oversight — so that money built through hard work reaches the ground as real, accountable progress.

What Makes Us Different

We are not a construction company, not a contractor marketplace, and not a fund manager. We are an independent oversight and decision-support service — a trusted second set of eyes with no interest in your project except the truth of what we find there.

Our Values

What we stand for

⚖️

Independence

We have no affiliation with any contractor, supplier, or construction firm. We work for you, and only you.

🔍

Evidence Over Opinion

Every finding we report is backed by physical site evidence — photos, measurements, documentation. Not assumptions.

💬

Plain Language

We write reports for owners, not engineers. You should be able to read our report and immediately know what to do.

🤝

Respect for Owners

We treat every client's project as if it were our own home. We bring the same seriousness whether the budget is Ksh 2M or Ksh 50M.

🌍

Diaspora Awareness

We understand the unique pressures of building from abroad — the distance, the trust, the stakes. We serve you accordingly.

📈

Continuous Improvement

We are refining our processes, reports, and coverage constantly — to be the most reliable oversight service in East Africa.
