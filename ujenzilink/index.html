The Problem

Construction in Kenya is built on trust. That trust is often broken.

Most owners discover problems too late — after money is gone, stages are incomplete, or work is done badly. The systems meant to protect you often don't work at all.

🚨

Money released before work is done

Contractors request payment for stages not yet complete. Without independent verification, most owners pay anyway.

⚠️

No visibility when you're far away

Diaspora and busy owners rely on phone calls and WhatsApp photos — neither gives a real picture of what's on site.

🏗️

Quality problems found too late

Substandard materials, hidden shortcuts, and shoddy workmanship only surface after the walls are up or the roof is on.

📑

No record of what was agreed

Verbal agreements, lost receipts, and vague BOQs leave owners with no leverage when things go wrong.

💸

Budget overruns with no explanation

Projects go over budget without clear reasons. Most owners don't know how to question contractor spending.

🤷

No one on your side

Engineers are often hired by the contractor. Lawyers only help after damage is done. You need oversight before things go wrong.

The Solution

A trusted second eye on every stage of your build.

UjenziLink is an independent oversight and decision-support service. We visit your site, assess the work, review your payment plans, and give you a clear, honest report — so you can make better decisions with your money.

We don't hold your funds. We don't work for your contractor. We work only for you.
