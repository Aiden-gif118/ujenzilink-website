Legal Disclaimer

Please read this disclaimer carefully before engaging UjenziLink's services.

Advisory Nature of Reports

All reports, assessments, recommendations, and communications produced by UjenziLink are advisory in nature. They are intended to support your decision-making as a project owner. They do not constitute engineering certifications, legal advice, financial advice, or any form of professional certification regulated by a statutory body.

No Engineering Certification

UjenziLink is not a licensed engineering firm. Our site reviewers are trained oversight professionals, not registered structural engineers. Our reports describe what is visible and accessible at the time of the site visit and do not certify structural adequacy, compliance with building regulations, or fitness for purpose of any construction work.

No Financial or Legal Advice

Payment recommendations included in UjenziLink reports are intended to inform, not instruct. They are based on observed site conditions at a specific point in time. UjenziLink does not provide legal advice regarding construction contracts, disputes, or contractor obligations. For legal matters, consult a qualified advocate.

Limitation of Site Visibility

Site reviews are limited to what is visible, accessible, and observable during a single visit. UjenziLink cannot assess concealed elements, structural integrity beyond visual indicators, or conditions that develop after the review date. Reports reflect a point-in-time snapshot.

Client Decision Responsibility

All decisions made by the client in response to UjenziLink reports remain the sole responsibility of the client. UjenziLink is not liable for outcomes arising from decisions made on the basis of our reports, including but not limited to contractor disputes, financial losses, or structural issues.

No Contractor Relationship

UjenziLink's engagement with a client does not create any contractual, employment, or advisory relationship with the client's contractor(s), subcontractors, or any other construction professionals on the project.
